PARICHAY — INTRODUCTION

Sacred Shores of the Sarayu

Nestled at the sacred Guptarghat on the banks of the Sarayu river in Ayodhya — the birthplace of Lord Ram — Harismriti Temple stands as a timeless witness to devotion, history, and the undying spirit of Sanatan Dharma.

"Where the Sarayu whispers eternally and the divine presence never fades — Harismriti awakens once more."

The temple revival initiative is coordinated under the auspices of INTACH – Ayodhya Chapter, guided by Convenor Ms. Manjula Jhunjhunwala, blending conservation expertise with devotional intent to restore this sacred site to its former glory.

SACRED GARDENS

Native medicinal and sacred plants — Tulsi, Neem, Peepal — cultivated as living offerings to nature and deity alike.

SOLAR ENERGY

Renewable solar power illuminating the sanctum, reducing the temple's carbon footprint while maintaining the sacred glow.

सौर ऊर्जा
💧

WATER CONSERVATION

Rainwater harvesting and eco-friendly waste management honoring the Sarayu's sanctity and the Guptarghat environment.

जल संरक्षण
♻️

ZERO PLASTIC

A plastic-free zone with biodegradable alternatives for prasad, flowers, and all ritual materials within the premises.

प्लास्टिक मुक्त
🪔

ECO DIYAS

Traditional clay diyas and natural beeswax candles replace synthetic lighting, preserving heritage aesthetics and ecology.
